**Full description** (max 4000)
```
Two worlds. One runner.

Shadow Runner is a fast, clever runner where you play two versions of the same character at the same time. Your physical self races through a bright, colourful city at the top of the screen – and your shadow runs through a dark, glowing shadow world below. One tap swaps which of you is real.

Walls stop your physical self, but your shadow phases straight through them. Bright light destroys your shadow, but your physical self runs safely beneath it. Read what is coming in both worlds, switch at the right moment and keep running.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap anywhere to switch between your physical self and your shadow
• Swipe up to jump, swipe down to slide
• Collect coins in the reality world and crystals in the shadow world
• Pull levers, step on plates and find keys – what you do in one world changes the other
• Reach the great portal at the end of each level and earn up to three stars

FEATURES
• 30 handcrafted levels in 6 worlds: First Light, Parallel Park, Neon City, Clockwork District, Sunken Temple and The Eclipse
• Cross-world puzzles: a lever above opens a gate below, a shadow plate builds a bridge above, a street lamp floods the shadow world with light
• Lasers, trains, gears, pendulums, collapsing stones, light beams, portals and eclipse zones where both of you are real at once
• Interactive tutorial that teaches everything in under two minutes
• Forgiving checkpoints and lives, with hints when you get stuck
• A new Daily Challenge every day, mixing two worlds into one run
• Endless mode – how far can you get?
• Unlock runner costumes, shadow forms, trails, switch effects and portal designs (cosmetic only)
• Generated music and sound effects, with haptic feedback

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Works completely offline – no account, no sign-up, no data collected
• No ads and no purchases
• Accessibility: left-handed mode, high-contrast mode, reduced motion, large touch targets and text labels on every colour cue
• Touch, mouse and keyboard controls

Switch. Jump. Slide. Run between the worlds.
```
