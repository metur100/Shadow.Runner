# 3D model credits

Every model in this folder is by **Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com), released under
**CC0 1.0 (public domain)**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Credit isn't
required, but we're happy to give it.

They were downloaded from poly.pizza and optimised with `scripts/optimize-models.mjs`, which
simplifies meshes, resizes textures to WebP at most 512 px and applies meshopt compression. Some are
recoloured in code; the hoodie, for example, becomes the costume colour.
